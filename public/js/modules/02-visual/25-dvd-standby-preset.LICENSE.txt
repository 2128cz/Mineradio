Visual preset 20「DVD待机」(DVD STANDBY) — reference port, licence UNVERIFIED
==========================================================================

POSTURE
-------
This build contains a port of a Shadertoy shader. Nothing containing this preset may be
distributed until the author confirms the terms.

REFERENCE
---------
Shadertoy view "wtcSzN" — https://www.shadertoy.com/view/wtcSzN
Title "DVD". The source was supplied by the requester as a single Image pass. The page did not
expose a licence header we could read. The fragment is ported as written into
DVD_SOURCE_FS (GLSL3): the DVD-logo SDF (dvd / dvd_v / dvd_c / dvd_d), the bouncing cell walk
(calcHitPos), the mirrored tile reflections (flip), the wavefront/ripple normal bump
(drawHit / drawReflectedHit / ref), the inverted + lit surface, the ordered dither and the gamma
curve.

Constants kept from the reference: the logo size 0.1, the 9:16 direction (45° on a 16:9 screen),
ripple centre at the last cell boundary, frequency 2, bump 0.1, dither 0.005, gamma 1/1.5 and the
lighting coefficients (5×diffuse + 0.2, 5×specular). Two defaults differ: the hit history is 10
instead of 5 and the ripple fade distance is 12 instead of 3, because the reference's rings
disappeared after one bounce; both are knobs.

CHANGES FROM THE SUPPLIED FRAGMENT
----------------------------------
- The pass compiles as GLSL3, so the output is `out vec4 outColor` and the supplied `mainImage`
  is called from a `main()`; the DEBUG branches are removed.
- The noise channel's `iChannelResolution[0]` is our generated 512×512 RGBA noise texture
  (`uNoiseRes` carries its size); it wraps, so the supplied `fragCoord / iChannelResolution`
  lookup is unchanged.
- The reference's `move = dir * iTime / 1.5` becomes `move = dir * uTravel`. The JS side advances
  `uTravel` continuously or, on a live beat onset, dashes it to the next cell boundary with an
  ease-in curve (`dashBeats`, `dashPower`). The dash uses only the live beat flag and the live beat
  gap, never a pre-analysed beat map, and resolves the next wall from the current playable
  rectangle, so resizing the logo or the window re-aims the dash.
- The rings do not inherit that dash: each wall crossing gets a birth time on a separate ripple
  clock advanced at the base speed, and the shader reads those ages from `uHitAges[16]`. A ring
  therefore keeps expanding at the normal rate while the logo rushes to the wall.
- A live beat additionally pulses the logo (scale + a short cyan halo) through `uBeat`; a wall
  impact briefly brightens the same halo through `uImpact`. These are original additions, exposed
  as `beatPunch` and `impactFlash`, and they deliberately do not re-time, tint or flash the
  background rings or the wall. The rings' relief fades with age through `heightDecay`; their
  colour can outlive the relief.
- The background surface is not the reference's inverted spectrum wall. The app's current cover
  texture is sampled as the flat water colour; the height gradient/normal refracts that lookup,
  and the steeper the slope the more a blurred copy of the cover is used (a 256×256 canvas rebuilt
  from the cover when it or the blur radius changes). The reference's `spectrum`/`pal` palette and
  its inverted colour pass are therefore not used. Knobs: `refract`, `coverBlur`.
- The reference's hardcoded direction is `uAngle` (default 45°, identical on 16:9), the logo
  scale, hit count, ripple range/frequency/life, bump, dither, gamma and a lighting multiplier are
  knobs; their defaults are the reference values except the history/life noted above.
- The reference is opaque to Shadertoy; our fragment forces alpha 1 so a full-screen pass cannot
  punch a translucent hole in the app's canvas.
- The app's particles and stage lyrics are suppressed: the reference composition is the whole
  picture and no lyric layer was added on top.
