Visual preset 19「故障艺术」(GLITCH ART) — reference-based components, licence UNVERIFIED
===========================================================================================

POSTURE
-------
This build contains components derived from a Shadertoy fault-art shader. Nothing containing this
preset may be distributed until the author confirms the terms.

REFERENCE
---------
Shadertoy view "lfscD7" — https://www.shadertoy.com/view/lfscD7
The page returned 403 from this machine; the requester supplied Common, Buffer A, Buffer B and the
Image pass. Common and Buffer A are ported verbatim into GLITCH_SOURCE_FS (GLSL3), with the
reference's #defines turned into uniforms whose defaults equal the reference literals. The Image
pass is adapted into GLITCH_COMPOSITE_FS (bloom + downsampled Buffer A at 0.6 + pow(0.8) +
mix(0.1,0.9) + vignette). The bloom is our two-pass approximation of Buffer B's separable down pass
plus the Image up pass. Constants:

  kVignetteStrength 0.5, kVignetteScale 0.6, kVignetteExponent 3.0, bloom weight 0.6,
  pow 0.8, mix(0.1, 0.9), kScreenDownsample 3, kAntiAlias 2, kTurns 7, kNumRipples 5,
  kSpeed 0.15, kZoom 0.35, kStaticFrequency 0.1, kVDisplaceFrequency 0.07,
  kHDisplaceFrequency 0.25, kColourQuantisation 5.

Differences from the reference: the passes compile as GLSL3 (uint/bit ops and texelFetch), the
reference blue-noise iChannel0 is replaced by a generated 1024x1024 white-noise texture (the same
Rand(sampler) contract), the default AA is 2 instead of 5 and iterations 1 instead of 2 for live
performance, and the bloom is a two-pass kernel approximation rather than the exact Buffer B/Image
pair. The centre hexagon logo and the glitched lyric plates are original additions, not in the
reference.

CHANGES FROM THE SUPPLIED FRAGMENT
----------------------------------
- `texelFetch(iChannel1, ivec2(xy)/kScreenDownsample, 0)` becomes a nearest texture2D sample at the
  centre of the matching low-res texel (uLowRes is that texture's size; the cell is clamped).
- `SeparableBlurUp(...)` is the separate two-pass bloom outlined above; the composite then fetches
  the finished bloom once.
- Every constant is a knob; the source/presentation uniforms are listed in GLITCH_SCHEMA and are
  also the audio connection points (audioDrive, audioGlitch, audioTear, audioBloom, beatPulse read
  the 8-band audio frame and the beat pulse).
- The app's particle layer is suppressed (the reference presentation is the whole picture). An
  original centre hexagon logo and glitched lyric plates were added on top of the presentation: the
  current lyric and its translation are rasterised to canvas textures and run through the same
  fault primitives (screen-space block shift, moving tear band, chromatic split, scanline), with the
  split fringes taking the logo's style hue. They are not part of the reference.
