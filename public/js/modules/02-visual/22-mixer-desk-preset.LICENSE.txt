What was changed by the port:
  - the reference body is kept as written; a few constants became uniforms: camera pan / sway /
    roll / fov, grid cell scale, level gain, march steps, shadow steps, max distance, and the
    density of inverted black/white panels; the reference literal .1 is the value that reproduces
    the reference, because the knob is now that share directly - 0 inverts no panel, 1 inverts
    every one - and a second knob randomises which panels sit near the boundary without moving the
    share. The inversion itself no longer rewrites the material polarity the reference did; it
    trades the plate colour with the engraving colour, so an inverted cell stays high contrast;
  - one volumetric term was added: the crevice between two plates holds a column of fog that the eye
    ray integrates along its path, so a nearer panel occludes a seam behind it. It is gated by the
    signed height difference between the two plates, so the light spills onto whichever sits lower
    and a flush seam stays dark;
  - the plate stagger is no longer the reference's per-cell random phase. The plates ride one shared
    sine whose amplitude, wavelength and phase are knobs, and that sine's clock is advanced from the
    song's beat period (one crest per beat, re-anchored to the world origin on each kick, with a
    short speed surge), so the desk lifts in travelling ridges rather than independent bumps;
  - the two plate colours are mixed towards the song's theme colour, and towards the colour 120
    degrees round the hue wheel from it for the inverted panels; the reference's own .9 and .04
    literals stay as the zero end of that mix, so at 0 the field is the reference greyscale;
  - the fragment writes alpha 1.0 so the post pass can copy it;
  - iChannel0 is fed an audio-driven level table (8 bands x 8 history steps).
